Before you start

Do I need all my content ready?

No. Share what you have now and tell us what is still missing. Your project chat keeps everything together.

Are hosting and a domain included?

Your quote will specify what is included. Hosting, domain ownership and ongoing costs should be agreed before launch.

How many changes can I request?

Your quote lists the included revision rounds. Additional changes can be discussed in the project chat.

When will my website be ready?

Share a preferred deadline in the brief. We will agree a delivery schedule after reviewing the project scope.

Can you maintain the website after delivery?

Ask for a maintenance arrangement. Scope, response times and fees can be agreed separately.
